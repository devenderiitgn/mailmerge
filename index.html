IITGN Mail Merge

Send personalised emails to many people at once, from your own IITGN Gmail. Upload an Excel sheet, write one message, and everyone gets their own copy with their name and details filled in.

Open Mail Merge Download sample Excel sheet

For @iitgn.ac.in and @alumni.iitgn.ac.in accounts

How to use it

  1. Prepare an Excel sheet. The first row holds column names, for example Email, Name, Batch. Not sure how? Download the sample sheet, replace the rows with your own people, and add or rename columns as you like.
  2. Open Mail Merge and upload the sheet.
  3. Write your subject and message. Click a column name to insert it, like {{Name}}.
  4. Check the preview, then send a test to yourself.
  5. Click Send to everyone. Download the report to see who got it.

First time only

Google asks for permission to send email from your account:

  1. Click Review permissions and pick your IITGN account.
  2. If you see "Google hasn't verified this app", click Advanced, then Go to IITGN Mail Merge.
  3. Click Allow.
Emails go out from your own account and show in your Gmail Sent folder. You can send about 1,500 emails a day on an IITGN account. Signed in to several Google accounts? Use an incognito window so the right one is used.
